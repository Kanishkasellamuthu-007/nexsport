What happens next

Room lifecycle
  • The room opens with a short join code and nearby players are notified using the ground coordinates.
  • The room closes automatically when it is full or one hour before kick-off.
  • On close, the greedy zig-zag algorithm splits everyone into two Elo-balanced teams.
  • After the match you upload the result, statistics and peer feedback — ratings update from there.
